# Business edition demo

- [ ] Open **Overview** in the Nimbu Living support workspace and point out the seven-day trend and open refund incident.
- [ ] Open **Live lab**, choose **Simulate traffic**, keep 30 messages and 35% going wrong, then start the simulation.
- [ ] Watch sent, answered wrong, incidents opened, and emails sent update. Open the new refund incident when it appears.
- [ ] Open **Mailpit inbox** and inspect the new-incident email, including its example conversation, likely cause, and estimated cost.
- [ ] In the incident, run **Try fixes** and show **Search current articles only** passing with reused-step statistics.
- [ ] Choose **Verify on all conversations** and wait for the bulk verification result and tokens-saved total.
- [ ] Return to Mailpit and open the **Fix verified** email.
- [ ] Resolve the incident with an optional note.
- [ ] Open **Evaluation**, select **Support conversations**, and close on the cross-domain result.
